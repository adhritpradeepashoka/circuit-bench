# Attribution

## Head model (`src/assets/head.glb`)

"3D human head scan" by **celer** on Thingiverse, https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:4245056,
licensed under Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY).

Changes made by CircuitBench: cropped below the neck, rotated into the app's axes (x right, y nose, z up),
reduced from about 458,000 to 36,000 triangles, and recentred and scaled to units of 100 mm.
The electrode positions in `src/core/head-sites.generated.js` were computed on that mesh.

## Robotics example parts
`public/robot-parts/kame32/` — the SG90 variants of the Kame32 quadruped's printed parts by Javier Isabel (JavierIH), https://github.com/JavierIH/kame32, licensed GPL-3.0 (licence text included in that folder). Shipped unmodified as example parts for the Assembly tab; `index.json` is generated by `scripts/make-example-parts.mjs`.

`public/robot-parts/kame32/servo-sg90.stl` — SG90 micro servo model (`Electrical Parts/Servos/SG-90/Servo-SG90.stl`) from the FreeCAD parts library, https://github.com/FreeCAD/FreeCAD-library, an open-source community library. The library does not state a licence for each part; if its author objects, it will be removed. Unmodified. The two ear holes in `index.json` were added by hand (27.4 mm apart, 2 mm); the 9 g mass is the manufacturer's figure.
