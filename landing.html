What you can do

One bench for the whole idea.

Most simulators stop at the circuit. CircuitBench carries on to the code, the body and the test.

🔌

Circuits that behave

Drag parts, draw wires, press Run. LEDs light, motors spin, sensors read, and a wrong connection shows up as one.

💻

Real firmware

Write Arduino C++ (or snap blocks together) and it compiles and runs on an emulated chip. Not a toy language.

🤖

A robotics workspace

Circuit, 3D assembly and a physics test in one place, so an arm, a rover or a drone is designed as a whole.

✨

Any part, on request

Search a part by name. The AI finds its datasheet and photo, then builds it with the right pins, size and sprite.

🧪

Learn by trying

Break things safely. Every wire, resistor and sketch is yours to change, with nothing to burn out.

🏫

Made for classes

Teachers make a class, share a code and follow students' progress. Students just join and build.

How it works

From idea to running in minutes.

No setup, no downloads, no parts to wait for.

Pick what to build

Start a circuit, a robot or an example. Search any part you need.

Wire it and write the code

Run, test, improve

Press Run, watch it work, fix what doesn't, and save it to come back to.
